The v2 contact sheet covers representative colors across all six families: T-shirt, long sleeve, hoodie, mug, cap, and key-ring water bottle. The source-kit tree contains 54 front-face cutouts with non-empty alpha bounds and no empty alpha assets.

The checkerboard apparel sheet confirms that the T-shirt color set has clean transparent boundaries without the pale rectangular/wedge background visible in the old live screenshot. Long-sleeve and hoodie assets preserve intended arm openings, cuffs, hoods, cords, pockets, and hems; the checkerboard shows those openings as transparency rather than opaque white background. No extra full-frame garment layer is present in the v2 source files.

The v2 product sheet also confirms that mug handles and rims, cap brims, and water-bottle ring-style caps remain part of the product silhouette. This is source-kit evidence only; live acceptance still requires deployment and browser checks after the resolver migration.
